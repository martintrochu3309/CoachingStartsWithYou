Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Coaching Starts With You (“the app”) is run by Daily Xcellence (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, why, who can see it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

IN SHORT

  • We collect your name, email, gender, age range, organization and your check answers, so your results are kept between checks.
  • Who can see your answers: you, the Coaching Starts With You team at Daily Xcellence and, if you joined through your organization’s link, your organization’s leader.
  • We don’t sell your information, show adverts or send marketing emails from the app.
  • We keep your information for 12 months after your programme ends, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Questions or requests: info@dailyxcellence.com

1. Who we are

The app is provided by Daily Xcellence. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, contact us at info@dailyxcellence.com.

This policy covers the app only. The Daily Xcellence website and the Pocket Mirror have their own privacy policies.

2. What we collect

When you sign up

  • First name and last name
  • Email address (used to sign you in with a 6-digit code)
  • Gender (including “Prefer not to say”)
  • Age range (for example 25–34)
  • Your organization, if you joined through your organization’s link

When you use the app

  • Your answers to the five questions at each check (Week 2, Week 4 and Week 6), and the score, A / B / C result and roadblock worked out from them
  • Your written answers to three short reflection questions (what you do well, when you fall back into a habit you want to change, and what you will do differently), on workshop day and at each check
  • The actions you write (“When…, I will…”) and the choices you make about them (for example keep it, or make it smaller)
  • The dates of your sign-up, your actions and your checks

On your device

The app saves a copy of your results and your sign-in on your phone or computer, so it works with a weak signal and you don’t have to sign in every time. We don’t use advertising cookies or analytics trackers in the app.

3. Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your results between checks, on any device.
  • To show you your results, tips and next check.
  • To give the Coaching Starts With You team, and your organization’s leader if you joined through their link, an overview of how coaches are progressing, so they can support you and improve the programme.
  • To understand, using combined and anonymous figures, how well the programme works and how to improve it.

For readers in the UK or EU: we use your information because it is needed to provide the programme you signed up for, and for our legitimate interest in running and improving it.

4. Who can see your information

  • You.
  • The Coaching Starts With You team at Daily Xcellence, who run the programme.
  • Your organization’s leader, if you joined through your organization’s link (for example a link for your club or department). They can see your name, your answers at each check (including your written answers) and the actions you write.

Other coaches cannot see your information. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

5. Services that store it for us

We use a small number of trusted providers. They only handle your information to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase: stores your account and results, and handles sign-in (servers in the USA).
  • Resend: sends the emails with your 6-digit sign-in code and your check reminders.
  • Netlify: hosts the app.

6. Emails

The app emails you your sign-in codes and, when each check opens (Week 2, Week 4 and Week 6), one reminder that it’s ready. That’s all. We do not send marketing emails from the app. If you choose to sign up to the Pocket Mirror or other Daily Xcellence products, they will ask for your permission separately.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for 12 months after your programme ends (your Week 6 check), then delete it or make it fully anonymous. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your account and all your answers;
  • answer any question about how your information is used.

Email info@dailyxcellence.com from the email address you signed up with. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Keeping it safe

The app uses encrypted connections. Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to your email, so there is no password to steal. Each coach can only see their own results, and access to everyone’s results is limited to the people named in section 4.

10. Who the app is for

The app is for adult coaches aged 18 and over. It is not meant for children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is important, for example who can see your answers, we will tell you in the app.
